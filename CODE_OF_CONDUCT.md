# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want ConsensusTree AI to be a respectful, useful space for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone participating in project discussions, issues, pull requests, and community spaces is expected to act with care and respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive when discussing ideas, code, and review findings.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and focus criticism on the work.
- Respect privacy. Do not post credentials, personal information, private source code, or confidential data.
- Follow the project's security reporting guidance in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not publish exploitable details or secrets in public issues.
- Accept that maintainers may decline changes that do not fit the project's scope or safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this Code of Conduct. To report a concern privately, contact the repository owner through the GitHub profile linked from this repository. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
